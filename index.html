Our Story

Just Me

Before you came into my life, I spent a long time feeling lost. I kept moving forward because that's what you're supposed to do, but deep down I didn't really know where I was going or what I was searching for.

The Quiet Days

Most days felt the same. I would wake up, do what I had to do, and go back to sleep. Nothing was truly wrong, but something always felt missing.

Overthinking

I spent a lot of time in my own head. Thinking about the future, thinking about mistakes, thinking about things I couldn't change. Sometimes it felt impossible to switch my mind off.

Searching

I wanted happiness, purpose, and something meaningful in my life. I just didn't know where I would find it, and i never knew if i actually would find it at all.

Carrying On

Even when things were difficult, I kept going. I learned how to hide my struggles behind a smile and convince myself everything was fine. Because in my mind, it was easier to carry it then causing trouble.

Hoping

Part of me always believed there had to be something better waiting ahead. I didn't know what it was, but I hoped one day I would find it.

Almost There

Looking back now, it feels strange knowing how close I was to meeting the person who would change so much for me. Make me feel alive again.

The Last Chapter Before You

I didn't know it then, but the lonely chapter of my life was coming to an end...

Then I Met Her

Everything Changed

You didn't arrive with some dramatic moment. Somehow, little by little, life simply started feeling brighter. Just talking to you on the phone somehow healed me more, every text you made.

A Reason To Smile

You make me smile more than you probably realise. Even on difficult days, thinking about you can completely change my mood. Thinking of how with everyone in this world, you chose me.

Feeling Understood

For the first time in a long time, I felt understood. I didn't feel like I had to hide parts of myself or pretend to be someone else. I felt seen, heard, and valued for who I really am.

The Best Part Of My Day

No matter what happens, hearing from you is still one of the things I look forward to most. Seeing your name pop up on my screen makes my day feel complete. Makes me feel cared for.

A Better Version Of Me

You've inspired me to become a better person. Not because you asked me to, but because you make me want to be. You make me want to be the best version of myself i could be.

Gratitude

Every day, I am grateful that our paths crossed. My life would be completely different if they hadn't, and sometimes i think what i would have done if i never met you.

Happiness

The truth is simple: you make me happy. Not for a moment, not occasionally, but every single day. Every single day i could be the most depressed i've ever been, and you still manage to make me smile, just existing and being yourself.

Thank You

So Thank you for your kindness, your patience, your heart, your smile, and for simply being you. You changed my life in ways I don't think words will ever fully explain, and im the most grateful man i could ever be.

The End... Or Maybe Just The Beginning

If someone had told me years ago that one person could change my life this much, I probably wouldn't have believed them.

Yet here you are.

You turned ordinary days into days I look forward to. You brought light into places that had been dark for far too long. You gave me reasons to smile when I had forgotten how.

You became my favourite person, my safest place, and one of the greatest gifts life has ever given me.

No website, no letter, and no amount of words could ever fully explain how grateful I am for you.

But if there is one thing I hope you take away from all of this, it's this:

Thank you for finding me.
Thank you for staying.
And thank you for being you.

I love you.
